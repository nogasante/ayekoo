RURAL WHOLESALE PRICES IN GHANA, 2024 (GH CEDIS PER TONNE)

These are annual nominal weighted average rural wholesale prices for 2024, the
most recent year in the source. They are national averages across the year, not
a price for today and not a price at any particular market.

Cassava: GH 5,044.56 per tonne in 2024.
Yam: GH 9,975.71 per tonne in 2024.
Plantain: GH 7,556.76 per tonne in 2024.
Cocoyam: GH 13,893.8 per tonne in 2024.
Maize: GH 9,285.91 per tonne in 2024.
Sorghum: GH 8,761.91 per tonne in 2024.
Groundnut: GH 16,792.6 per tonne in 2024.
Millet: GH 9,797.51 per tonne in 2024.
Rice: GH 11,164.9 per tonne in 2024.

HOW PRICES HAVE MOVED SINCE 2015

Nominal maize prices rose from GH 1,421.69 per tonne in 2015 to GH 9,285.91 per
tonne in 2024. Yam rose from GH 1,220.63 to GH 9,975.71 over the same period.
Cassava rose from GH 526.86 to GH 5,044.56.

Much of that rise is inflation rather than real gain. The source also publishes
real prices at constant value in its Table 7.2: in real terms maize moved from
GH 941.45 per tonne in 2015 to GH 4,116.09 in 2024, and cassava from GH 348.89
to GH 2,236.07. Nominal figures overstate how much better off a farmer is.

WHAT THESE PRICES ARE NOT

They are annual national averages for 2024. They are not current market prices,
they are not farm-gate prices, and they do not reflect what any particular
market or buyer pays on a given day. Prices vary by region, by season, and
sharply between harvest and lean periods. A farmer deciding when to sell should
treat these as a reference point, not a quote.
