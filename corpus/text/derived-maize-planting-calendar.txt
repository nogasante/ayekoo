MAIZE PLANTING PERIODS IN GHANA, BY AGRO-ECOLOGICAL ZONE AND SEASON

Ghana's south has two maize seasons, a major and a minor. The Guinea and Sudan
savannah zones of the north have one season only.

MAJOR SEASON PLANTING PERIODS

Forest zone: plant Early March to end of April.
Transition zone (forest-savannah transition): plant Mid-March to end of April.
Coastal savannah zone: plant End of March to end of April.
Guinea savannah and Sudan savannah zones (northern Ghana): plant End of May to
early July.

MINOR SEASON PLANTING PERIODS

Forest zone: plant Mid July to early September.
Transition zone: plant Mid July through August to early September.
Coastal savannah zone: plant Mid July to Mid August.
Guinea savannah and Sudan savannah zones: no minor season. The source records
this as "Not applicable" — northern Ghana has a single rainy season, so there is
no second maize crop.

WHICH ZONE IS A PLACE IN

The only region-to-zone statement corroborated by a source in this corpus is
that the Sudan Savannah Zone includes districts in the Upper East Region.

A fuller mapping of Ghana's regions to agro-ecological zones is NOT recorded
here, because no document in this corpus states one. A farmer naming a town or
region rather than a zone therefore cannot be resolved automatically yet. This
is a known gap, and it must be filled from a citable source rather than from
general knowledge.
