INTRODUCTION 
This quarterly bulletin seeks to assess food stock levels, pattern of drawdowns of grain stocks (Maize, Millet, Sorghum, Rice, Soyabean, Groundnut and Cowpea) in households and 
markets in the AGRA Zone of Interest (ZOI) in Northern Ghana. Cereals and legumes serve as staple food and cash crops in many households in the ZOI. As such, accurate 
knowledge of the quantity of grain stocks, drawdowns in households, as well as volumes and prices of grain stocks in markets is crucial for policy formulation on food security.  
 
 
METHODOLOGY 
• Households are categorized by wealth status (Better off, Middle, Poor, Very Poor). Thirty (30) households are selected to represent each district, proportional to the wealth 
distribution of the district. Selected households are visited quarterly to assess their levels of stocks. 
• Data was collected in all major markets in the districts to assess the stock levels and prices in markets.

1. Generally, prices of grains per kilo decreased in Q3_2025, compared to
